Website Biography

Hello i am student in Moringa welcome to my photographic website where i indulg in the war of the centuries.n exploration of the geopolitical, technological, and human shifts that defined the 20th century through the lens of global conflict. This project analyzes the interconnected timelines of World War I and World War II, examining how industrial-scale warfare dismantled empires, accelerated technological innovation, and established the modern international order.

After the War

The slow, quiet art of healing a nation. This segment explores the profound transition from total war to reconstruction, documenting how nations rebuilt from rubble, societies healed from deep trauma, and a new international order emerged from the ashes.

The Blueprint of Resilience

Ultimately, this portfolio serves as a visual bridge between the devastation of total war and the enduring power of human resilience.the raw chaos of global conflict with the methodical, decades-long process of reconstruction, these images capture more than just historical milestones—they document the survival of the human spirit. Through the preservation of both physical scars and architectural renewal, this collection honors the memory of the past while offering a profound reflection on the fragile nature of peace.
